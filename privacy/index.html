Privacy Policy

MIDI Metronome, Ear Trainer, Circuits and Electronics · by Paweł Kowalewski · Last updated 27 September 2026

These apps do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your settings, presets, progress and saved work are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network

The apps work fully offline. All sounds, fonts and content are bundled with the app; nothing is downloaded from third-party servers while you use it.

Files you open

If you open a file (for example a MIDI file in MIDI Metronome, or a backup file), it is read on your device only and is not uploaded anywhere.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the apps are suitable for users of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support link on the app's App Store page.
