Privacy Policy

MIDI Metronome, Music Theory & Ear Training, Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanics and Acoustics · by Paweł Kowalewski · Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: these apps do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no newsletters and no tracking of any kind. Music Theory & Ear Training offers one optional in-app purchase (the full course), handled by Apple; we receive no payment or personal data. Nothing you do in the apps is sent to us or to anyone else.

Who is responsible

The apps are made and published by Paweł Kowalewski, an individual developer based in Poland (European Union). Because the apps process no personal data on any server, there is no data controller holding information about you. For questions, see Contact below.

What stays on your device

Your settings, presets, setlists, practice programs, progress and saved work are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear it at any time by removing the app.

Microphone (Music Theory & Ear Training only)

Music Theory & Ear Training asks for the microphone in the exercises where you sing, echo a melody, sing or play from the score, or clap a rhythm, to check the pitch and rhythm you produce. The sound is analysed on your device while the exercise runs; for clapping and playing from the score it is held in memory for a few seconds to be checked, then discarded. It is never saved or sent anywhere. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in the iPhone or iPad Settings; the other exercises work without it. No other app uses the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Files and moving progress

If you open a file (for example a MIDI file in MIDI Metronome, or a backup file), it is read on your device only. To move progress to another device, the learning apps create a code, link or file that you pass on yourself; it contains only your progress in the app, and it does not go through any server of ours.

Network

The apps work fully offline. All sounds, fonts and content are bundled with the app; nothing is loaded from third-party servers while you use it. There are no third-party SDKs, trackers or advertising networks inside the apps.

Apple and the App Store

Downloads and updates are handled by Apple. Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example, if you choose to share crash reports or analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple passes on anonymous, aggregated reports). We never receive your name, email address or any other personal details from Apple.

Children

The apps are suitable for all ages. Because there are no accounts, no communication features and no data collection, children can use them without sharing any personal information. There is nothing to sign up for.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl). Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete; all app data is on your own device and under your control.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if an app gains a feature that needs data, the new version will be published on this page with a new date before that feature is released.
